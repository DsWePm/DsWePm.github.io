A 3D rendering practice piece, published on my Bilibili channel. The title, 囹圄 (*lingyu*), means "prison".

![Lingyu](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1D841167Rs)

## Premise

In the near future, a quantum-based space-time camera is invented. The researchers hope to use it to uncover secrets from the distant past, but they see phenomena they can't explain…

## Production

| Part | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Bamboo forest, houses, chains and other assets | Houdini |
| Some assets | Quixel Bridge, 3d66 |
| Character | DAZ |
| Character's clothes (based on the official MD hanfu) | Marvelous Designer |
| Rain hitting the bamboo, rain on the leaves, the swinging chains | Houdini |
| Falling petals | Niagara |
| Rain and sky | Ultra Dynamic Sky |
| Cloth motion | Marvelous Designer |

- The rain on the bamboo leaves is quite dark in the frame and may be hard to see
- The bamboo forest's style was inspired by Leo Torres's [ArtStation piece](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/04oqvY)
